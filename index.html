About Us

A leading name in aluminium formwork for modern construction

TopCraft Aluminium Formwork System Pvt. Ltd. is a leading and trusted name in the manufacturing, design and supply of high-quality aluminium formwork systems for the modern construction industry.

We provide durable, efficient and reusable formwork solutions for residential, commercial and infrastructure projects. Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility and team of experienced engineers ensure that every project is completed on time with the highest standards of finishing.

“We don't just build formwork; we provide speed and strength to the foundation of modern construction.”

01 · Manufacturing Infrastructure

Machinery, skill and quality control under one roof

Our manufacturing unit is equipped with top-tier modern machinery, hydraulic presses, cutting and welding equipment. We have a skilled workforce and an in-house quality control system that ensures production meets international standards.

  • Hydraulic presses and CNC-assisted cutting
  • Dedicated welding and finishing bays
  • Skilled workforce trained for formwork fabrication
  • In-house QC bay with inspection checklists
02 · Our Key Products

Customised aluminium formwork panels

We offer a complete range of custom and standard aluminium formwork panels and accessories to meet diverse project requirements.

Wall & Slab Panels

Lightweight, high durability and delivering an excellent concrete finish — the backbone of the TopCraft monolithic system.

Beam & Column Formwork

Engineered to precise measurements for challenging structural designs — adjustable column boxes and beam soffits sized to your drawings.

03 · Key Benefits

Key benefits of TopCraft aluminium formwork

High Repetitions

Our formwork panels can be reused more than 200 times.

Rapid Cycle

Enables slab-to-slab casting within 4 to 7 days.

Smooth Finish

Minimises or eliminates the need for internal plastering.

Lightweight & Strong

Easy manual assembly on site without the need for heavy cranes.

Cost Effective

Significant savings in both labour costs and overall project time.
