Workflow

How a refurbishment job runs

1

Inspection

Panels are sorted, inspected and quoted against the work required.

2

Cleaning & Repair

Chemical cleaning, grinding, cutting and welding as needed.

3

Resizing & Lacquering

Panels are modified to size and re-lacquered for a fresh surface.

4

Palletising & Dispatch

Strapped, palletised and labelled for the trip back to your site.
