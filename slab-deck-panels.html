Slab / Deck Panels

Flat, fast, safe — every slab, every cycle

Overview

Deck formwork built for high-rise slab cycles

Slab panels and deck components are engineered to keep the floor plate flat and the cycle short. Panels interlock with wall panels, beams and columns to complete the monolithic pour.

  • Deck panels sized to your slab grid
  • I-stiffener for flat, straight soffits
  • Props and U-jacks for load transfer
  • MS jally for edge and beam soffit safety
Components

Everything on the slab

Deck Panel

Primary slab soffit panel, sized to your grid and edge conditions.

I-Stiffener

Stiffens deck panels against deflection and pour pressure.

Props

Adjustable props transfer slab loads to the floor below.

U-Jack

Holds beam bars and props at the correct height.

MS Jally

Safety mesh and edge support for open slab edges.

Beam Bar

Aligns beam soffit and sides within the slab set.

Typical Floor Cycle

Day 1 → Day 7

A representative 7-day slab-to-slab cycle for a typical floor using TopCraft deck panels and props. Actual cycle depends on crew, weather and structure.

Day 1Deck panels positioned; props set.
Day 2Beam bars & edge shutter fixed; MEP sleeves placed.
Day 3Reinforcement tied; cover blocks placed.
Day 4Slab pour and vibration.
Day 5Curing; props remain in place.
Day 6Props released; panels struck.
Day 7Panels cleaned & lifted; next cycle begins.
