Manufacturing & Technology

Where Precision is Manufactured

Our manufacturing unit is equipped with top-tier modern machinery, hydraulic presses, cutting and welding equipment — backed by a skilled workforce and in-house quality control that meets international standards.

Manufacturing Infrastructure

Modern Machinery, Skilled Hands

From hydraulic presses to precision cutting and welding stations, every stage is controlled under one roof.

  • Hydraulic presses for accurate panel forming
  • CNC and manual cutting stations
  • MIG welding with jigs for repeatability
  • In-house QC lab and inspection benches
  • Palletising and dispatch yard
Machinery

Equipment That Delivers Repeatability

Hydraulic Presses

Consistent panel forming with controlled pressure.

Cutting Stations

Accurate profile and panel cutting to drawing dimensions.

Welding Jigs

MIG welding with fixtures for repeatable strength.

QC Benches

Surface, hole and dimensional inspection before dispatch.

Workflow

From CAD to Dispatch

1. CAD / BIM Designing

Precise 3D models and layouts from client blueprints. Shell plans, layouts, elevations, BOQ and fabrication drawings prepared and approved.

2. Precision Cutting & Welding

Robotic and manual cutting and welding of high-grade aluminium alloys, using jigs for repeatable accuracy.

3. Quality Inspection

Rigorous checking of every panel's surface, holes and dimensions against approved fabrication drawings.

4. Packing & Dispatch

Strapped and palletised packaging for safe delivery, with accessories itemised and packed separately.

Quality Control

Every Panel Passes These Checks

Surface Finish

Uniform surface free from dents, scratches or weld spatter.

Hole Accuracy

Hole positions checked against fabrication drawings.

Dimensions

Length, width and squareness within tolerance.

Welding Strength

Visual and mechanical verification of weld integrity.

Flatness

Panel flatness measured to ensure smooth concrete finish.

Packing & Dispatch

Strapped, palletised and labelled for safe site delivery.
