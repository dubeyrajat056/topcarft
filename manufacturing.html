Our Plant

Manufacturing infrastructure

Our manufacturing unit is equipped with top-tier modern machinery, hydraulic presses, cutting and welding equipment. We have a skilled workforce and an in-house quality control system that ensures production meets international standards.

  • Hydraulic presses and CNC-assisted cutting
  • Dedicated welding and finishing bays
  • Skilled workforce trained for formwork fabrication
  • In-house QC bay with inspection checklists

In-house QC

Workflow

From design to dispatch

Step 1 · CAD / BIM Designing

Precise 3D models & layouts

We build 3D formwork models and layouts from your client blueprints, resolve clashes, and issue drawings for approval before any fabrication begins.

Step 2 · Precision Cutting & Welding

Robotic & manual fabrication

High-grade aluminium alloys are cut and welded to drawing — every panel carries a matching fabrication sheet through the line.

Step 3 · Quality Inspection

Rigorous checking on every panel

Surface finish, hole positions, dimensions, welds and flatness — all checked before a panel is allowed to move to packing.

Step 4 · Packing & Dispatch

Strapped and palletised for safe delivery

Panels are bundled, strapped and palletised with the right accessories so site installation can begin as soon as they are unloaded.

Quality Control

A checklist that never leaves the building

Every panel is inspected against a written checklist before dispatch. If it doesn't pass, it doesn't ship.

Request QC Documentation

Holes

Dimensions

Welding
