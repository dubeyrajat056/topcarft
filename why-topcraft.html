Why TopCraft

The Formwork Advantage

  1. Home
  2. Why TopCraft
01 · Design Expertise

CAD / BIM Precision from Blueprints

We convert your architectural and structural drawings into precise 3D formwork models, complete with layouts, elevations, BOQ and fabrication drawings.

  • Shell plan preparation & approval
  • Formwork layouts & elevations
  • Accessories list & layouts
02 · In-house Manufacturing

Full Control Over Quality & Delivery

Our manufacturing unit is equipped with modern machinery, hydraulic presses and cutting/welding equipment. Because everything happens in-house, we control quality and lead times.

  • High-grade aluminium alloys
  • Robotic and manual welding
  • Skilled workforce
03 · Quality Control

Every Panel. Every Dimension. Checked.

Our in-house QC team inspects surface finish, hole positions, dimensions, welding strength and flatness before dispatch.

  • Surface & finish inspection
  • Hole & groove accuracy
  • Dimensional tolerance checks
04 · Reusability & Economy

200+ Cycles. Long-Term Savings.

Aluminium formwork pays for itself across repeated floors, reducing material waste and labour dependency with each cycle.

  • 200+ reuses with consistent finish
  • Minimal plastering required
  • No heavy cranes — manual assembly
05 · On-site Support

We Stand with Your Team on Site

From mock-up supervision to first-cycle assembly, our engineers provide hands-on technical guidance so your crew achieves cycle targets safely.

  • Mock-up supervision
  • Training for site teams
  • After-sales support
06 · Safety

Engineered for Safe Working Conditions

Lightweight panels, integrated safety accessories and methodical assembly sequences reduce site risk and rework.

  • Lightweight, man-portable panels
  • Accessories designed for secure connections
  • Documented assembly procedures
