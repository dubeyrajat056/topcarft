Why TopCraft

Six reasons builders keep coming back

Design expertise, in-house manufacturing, strict quality control, reusability, on-site support and safety — engineered into every panel we ship.

Explore the Six
  1. Home
  3. About

01 · Design Expertise

Drawings that resolve problems before they reach site

Our engineers produce shell plans, formwork layouts, elevations, BOQ and fabrication drawings from your structural and architectural drawings.

  • CAD/BIM modelling with clash review
  • Formwork layouts and elevations signed off before fabrication
  • Accessories list & layouts supplied with every set
02 · In-house Manufacturing

Machinery, skill and quality control under one roof

Cutting, welding, hydraulic pressing and finishing are done in-house — which means predictable lead times and full traceability on every panel.

  • Modern cutting and welding equipment
  • Skilled workforce with formwork-specific experience
  • In-house QC system aligned to international standards
See the Plant
03 · Quality Control

Every panel checked before it leaves the plant

Surface finish, hole positions, dimensions, welding and flatness are verified on every panel — not sampled.

  • Dimensional accuracy within agreed tolerance
  • Hole and groove inspection against fabrication drawing
  • Welding integrity and flatness checks
04 · Reusability & Economy

200+ cycles — and a refurbishment line to extend it further

Aluminium formwork pays for itself across the project. When panels do wear, our refurbishment line cleans, resizes and re-lacquers them to extend service life.

  • 200+ reuse cycles with consistent finish
  • Refurbishment and resizing at a fraction of replacement cost
  • Real savings in labour, time and material waste
Refurbishment Services
05 · On-site Support

Our engineers stand next to yours

Mock-up supervision, first-cycle guidance and troubleshooting are part of the package — not an upsell.

  • Supervision for the mock-up flat
  • On-site setting of aluminium panels
  • Technical support during the first full floor cycle
06 · Safety

Light panels, sensible details, fewer risks

Panels are designed for manual handling — reducing crane dependency and the risk profile of your site.

  • Manual assembly with no heavy cranes
  • Pins, wedges and brackets designed for safe walking surfaces
  • Strapped and palletised packaging for safe unloading
