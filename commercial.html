Challenge · Solution · Benefits

Where commercial formwork gets hard

Challenge

Big spans, visible finishes

Commercial buildings often have larger spans and exposed concrete surfaces where every seam and finish line matters.

Solution

Panel layout that respects the finish

Beam soffit panels, deck panels and columns are laid out to hide seams and give the architect the finish they designed for.

Benefits

Exposed concrete that survives inspection

Off-form finish and repeatable panel geometry let exposed concrete stay exposed — reducing cover-up work and rework.

Coordinating MEP

Openings and sleeves engineered into the panel

Commercial buildings are MEP-heavy. TopCraft panels are fabricated with sleeve openings and coordination details already built in — so the pour doesn't have to be chased later.

  • Sleeve openings coordinated with MEP drawings
  • PVC sleeves supplied with the panel set
  • Beam bars and panel layouts coordinated with services
  • On-site technical guidance during first cycles
