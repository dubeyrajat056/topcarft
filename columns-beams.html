Overview

Columns & beams — where formwork is truly tested

Columns and beams carry the most variability in a building. Our system gives you adjustable column boxes for varying sections, beam soffits sized to span, and beam bars for alignment — all in high-grade aluminium alloy.

  • Adjustable column boxes for varying sections
  • Beam bar and BKS Patti for alignment and support
  • Soldier profiles stiffen tall column faces
  • Easy striking and reuse on the next floor
Components

What makes the system work

Adjustable Column Box

Sliding or bolted panels that adapt to different column sections and heights.

Beam Soffit Panels

Sized to your beam width and depth for flat, straight soffits.

Beam Bar & BKS Patti

Alignment and support hardware for beam sides and soffit.

Soldier Profile

Vertical stiffener for tall column and beam faces.

Corner Angles

ECA profiles for sharp, clean external corners.

Pins, Wedges & Tie Rods

Fast, safe assembly and disassembly — no special tools required.

Use Cases

Where columns & beams formwork wins

Residential towers

Repeatable column sections and beam layouts across typical floors — cycle time drops as crews get familiar with the set.

Commercial podiums

Large spans and varying beam depths; beam soffit panels sized to each span reduce waste and improve finish.

High-rise cores

Tall column faces stiffened with soldier profiles maintain alignment and pour quality on every cycle.

Infrastructure portals

Heavy structural elements supported with beam bars, jacks and props for safe, controlled pours.
