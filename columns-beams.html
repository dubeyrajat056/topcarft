Overview

Engineered for Challenging Structural Designs

TopCraft beam and column formwork is engineered to precise measurements for challenging structural designs — with an adjustable column concept that adapts to changing sizes across floors.

  • Adjustable column formwork
  • Beam bars and soldier profiles for alignment
  • Easy stripping and reuse
  • Compatible with wall and slab systems
