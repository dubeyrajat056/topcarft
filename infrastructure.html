Challenge · Solution · Benefits

Where infrastructure formwork demands more

Challenge

Heavy loads, unusual geometry

Bridges, piers, decks and public works often have non-standard shapes and much higher form pressures than typical building slabs.

Solution

Engineered to the drawing

We engineer custom panels, beam soffits and heavy props for your specific structure — not just standard panels adapted to fit.

Benefits

Safety and precision together

Reusable aluminium formwork brings repeatable geometry and safe assembly to projects where mistakes are costly.

Custom Engineering

Formwork engineered to the structure — not the shelf

Standard systems don't fit bridges and piers. Our team starts with your structural drawings and works backward to panel geometry, prop layout and pour sequence.

  • Custom panel geometry for non-standard sections
  • Heavy-load props, jacks and beam bars
  • Engineered pour sequence to control form pressure
  • Site-specific safety assessment and tooling
