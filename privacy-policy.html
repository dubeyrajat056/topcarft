Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TopCraft Aluminium Formwork System Pvt. Ltd. ("TopCraft", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects the information you provide when you use this website or contact us through it.

We are committed to handling your information responsibly. If you have any questions, contact us using the details at the end of this page.

What we collect

When you fill a form on this site, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • Company name and project location
  • Message content and attachments you choose to send

We also collect limited technical information automatically (IP address, browser type, pages visited) via our web server and analytics tools, if enabled.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotations
  • To prepare formwork layouts, drawings and estimates
  • To improve the website and our services
  • To comply with legal or regulatory obligations

Cookies

This site may use minimal cookies for functionality and analytics. If third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics) is enabled, it may set its own cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We may share it with:

  • Our own engineers and commercial team
  • Service providers (email, hosting, form handling) under confidentiality
  • Authorities, where required by law

Retention

We retain enquiry data for as long as necessary to respond and to maintain a record of our commercial communications, after which we delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Delete your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for future communication
