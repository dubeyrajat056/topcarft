Challenge · Solution · Benefits

What changes when the tower gets tall

Challenge

Cycle time compounds with height

Every extra day per floor becomes weeks over a 30-storey tower. Labour cost, crane time and financing all scale with the cycle.

Solution

Standard cycle, engineered layout

Aluminium panels and standard accessories keep the cycle predictable; on-site supervision tightens the first few floors.

Benefits

Every cycle saves days

Fewer plaster touch-ups, less crane time and no rebuild for changes — savings multiplied by the number of floors.

Cores, Cores, Cores

Cores drive the schedule on tall towers

Core walls and lift shafts set the pace for the whole tower. TopCraft wall panels and column boxes are engineered to keep the core on a predictable cycle — and the floor plates follow.

  • Wall panels engineered for tall core faces
  • Adjustable column boxes for varying sections
  • Slab/deck panels and props coordinated with core pour
  • Refurbishment extending panel life across phases
