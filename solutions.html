One System, Many Applications

What stays constant across every project

Whatever the building type, four things determine whether a formwork system works on site: cycle time, dimensional accuracy, finish quality and safety. TopCraft panels and accessories are designed against all four.

Predictable Cycle

Reusable panels, standard accessories and clear layouts keep each floor cycle on schedule.

Dimensional Control

Panel tolerances and layout discipline keep walls, columns and slabs where the drawings say.

Finish Quality

Off-form concrete that minimises plaster — critical for exposed and commercial finishes.

Safety

Lightweight manual assembly reduces crane work and manual handling risks.
