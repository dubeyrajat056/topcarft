Why residential towers choose aluminium formwork

Challenge

Repetitive slabs, tight schedules

Residential developers need every floor plate to match the one below — within days, not weeks — with a finish that won't need patching later.

Solution

One set, many floors

Aluminium formwork gives you 200+ reuses of the same walls, columns and slab panels — with layouts and elevations engineered to your grid.

Benefits

Faster handover, lower cost

4–7 day cycles, off-form finish and reduced labour translate into lower total cost and earlier occupancy.

Built for Repeatability

The same panel, the same floor, the same result

Residential projects live or die on repetition. Our panels are fabricated to your typical floor plan so every level matches — and the crew gets faster with every cycle.

  • Typical floor layouts engineered once, reused many times
  • Wall, column and slab panels coordinated with MEP openings
  • On-site supervision for the first cycle to lock in the routine
  • Refurbishment service to extend panel life across phases
