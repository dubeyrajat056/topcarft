Overview

Walls and slabs poured in a single operation

Aluminium formwork is a construction system for forming cast-in-place concrete structures. TopCraft's system enables walls and slabs to be poured in the same operation — increasing efficiency and producing an extraordinarily strong structure with an excellent concrete finish.

  • Monolithic wall + slab pour in one cycle
  • Off-form concrete finish — minimise or eliminate internal plastering
  • Manual assembly with no heavy cranes required
  • 200+ reuse cycles per panel set

How it Works

The four-step cycle

1

Set Out & Assemble

Kickers, wall panels and slab panels are assembled on the previous floor.

2

Fix Accessories

Tie rods, pins, wedges, brackets and props are installed and checked.

3

Pour & Cure

Walls and slab are poured in one operation; concrete is vibrated and cured.

4

Strike & Cycle

Panels are struck, cleaned and lifted to the next floor for the following cycle.
